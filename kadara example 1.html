    Help Me Choose

    Three decisions. Straight answers.

    Elevate your practice with our top-tier pellet trocar devices and hrt trocar kits crafted for unparalleled precision and patient comfort.

    Disposable or reusable

    Disposable Trocar KitsReusable Trocar Sets

    Reusable Trocar Sets are designed for repeated use and require autoclave sterilization between procedures. Disposable Trocar Kits are single use and offer a convenient ready to use option for practices that prefer a streamlined workflow.

    Stainless steel or titanium

    Stainless SteelTitanium

    Both options are durable, high quality choices. Titanium is lighter in weight and is often selected by practitioners who prefer a more premium feel in hand, while stainless steel remains a dependable, durable standard.

    3.2 mm or 4.5 mm

    3.2mm4.5mm

    In general, the 4.5 mm size is typically used for 200 mg pellets, while the 3.2 mm size is ideal for 150 mg pellets and smaller. If your practice has specific procedural preferences, our team can help guide you to the best fit.

    FAQ's

    Ordering, shipping and product questions.

    Still have questions? Contact kadara™ medical Customer Service for product guidance, ordering support, and account assistance.

    Do I need a kadara™ medical account to place an order?+

    Yes. A kadara™ medical account is required to place an order and access customer specific pricing, downloadable materials, and product details. Once your account is set up, ordering is easy. Simply add your products to cart, head to checkout, and you are on your way.

    Who is eligible to order from kadara™ medical?+

    Orders are limited to licensed medical practitioner offices, clinics, and compounding pharmacies. If you are unsure whether your facility qualifies, our team is happy to help confirm eligibility.

    What is included in a Disposable Trocar Kit?+

    Kadara disposable trocar kits include all the components needed for a patient first focused procedure. The only thing you need to provide is your lidocaine with epinephrine and pellets. Those items are not included in the kit.

    Do kadara’s Reusable Trocar Sets include accessories?+

    Accessories are sold separately. Our Add On Bundle includes the forceps, pellet cup, and sterilization tray for practices that want a complete supporting setup.

    Can I mix different products within a case?+

    No. Each case contains one product type only. This helps maintain consistency, inventory accuracy, and quality control from start to finish.

    What shipping options are available?+

    We offer multiple shipping options to fit your timeline, including Ground, 3 Day Express Saver, 2 Day, Standard Overnight, and Priority Overnight for select delivery areas.

    What is your standard shipping time?+

    Most orders arrive within 1 to 5 business days depending on your location and selected shipping method. Delivery timelines may vary slightly during holidays or high volume shipping periods.

    What is the shelf life of your products?+

    All products carry a 1 year shelf life from the manufacturer date listed on the packaging. We recommend storing products according to labeled instructions and rotating inventory as part of your normal practice workflow.

    Can you help me source pellets?+

    Yes. We are happy to provide referrals to trusted compounding pharmacies and outsourcing facilities based on your state. If you need direction, just ask and we will point you toward the right resource.
